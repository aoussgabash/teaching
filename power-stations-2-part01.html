Course Syllabus | مفردات المقرر

English

  1. Types of generating stations, characteristics and primary energy sources
  2. Hydro, gas, coal, nuclear, wind and other generation technologies
  3. Power plant equipment and connection diagrams
  4. Generating units, operating principles and structure
  5. Interconnection diagrams, auxiliary supply and short-circuit limitation
  6. Power plant operation and output control
  7. Coordinated operation, reliability, continuity and power quality
  8. Coordination and emergency automatic control
  9. Selection of generating and drive units and site planning
  10. Environmental protection, pollution reduction, design and installation
  11. Modeling, simulation and modern generation applications

Introduction | مقدمة

Electrical generation converts primary energy into electrical energy. This course covers generating technologies, unit construction, plant operation, grid connection, economic operation, environmental protection and modeling.

Modern extensions include photovoltaic and wind generation, battery energy storage, digital control and Python-based simulation.

تحول محطات التوليد مصادر الطاقة الأولية إلى طاقة كهربائية. يدرس المقرر تقنيات التوليد وبنية الوحدات وتشغيل المحطات وربطها بالشبكة والتشغيل الاقتصادي وحماية البيئة والنمذجة.
