COURSE 04

Programming in Electrical Power II

البرمجة في الطاقة 2

Advanced computational problem solving for electrical power engineering, building on MATLAB fundamentals acquired in Programming in Electrical Power I.

12 Lectures × 2 h12 Labs × 1.5 hMATLAB prerequisitePower Engineering Applications
Prerequisite | المتطلب السابق: Programming in Electrical Power I — MATLAB fundamentals: scripts, vectors and matrices, complex numbers, functions, loops, conditional statements and plotting.

Lectures | المحاضرات

12 two-hour lectures. Detailed pages, assessments and downloadable summaries will be activated progressively.

LECTURE 01 · 2 h

Computational Power Engineering & MATLAB Review

الحوسبة في هندسة الطاقة ومراجعة MATLAB

Engineering equations → algorithms; complex quantities, matrices, functions and plots.

LECTURE 02 · 2 h

Load Curves, Energy & Data Processing

منحنيات الحمل والطاقة ومعالجة البيانات

Daily load curves, energy, peak demand, average load and load factor.

LECTURE 03 · 2 h

AC Power, Phasors & Complex Power

الاستطاعة المتناوبة والشعاعات والاستطاعة العقدية

Phasors, instantaneous quantities and active, reactive and apparent power.

LECTURE 04 · 2 h

Three-Phase Systems

الأنظمة ثلاثية الطور

Balanced systems, star/delta relationships, power and visualization.

LECTURE 05 · 2 h

Power-Factor Correction

تحسين عامل الاستطاعة

Reactive-power compensation, capacitor sizing and parameter studies.

LECTURE 06 · 2 h

Synchronous Generator Computation

الحسابات البرمجية للمولدة المتزامنة

Internal EMF, load angle, excitation and operating characteristics.

LECTURE 07 · 2 h

Transmission Lines: Short & Medium Models

خطوط النقل: النماذج القصيرة والمتوسطة

Sending/receiving quantities, losses, efficiency and voltage regulation.

LECTURE 08 · 2 h

ABCD Parameters & Line Performance

ثوابت ABCD وأداء خطوط النقل

Matrix formulation, nominal-π model and transmission performance.

LECTURE 09 · 2 h

Power-Flow Foundations

أساسيات سريان الاستطاعة

Bus models, network equations, Y-bus and load-flow formulation.

LECTURE 10 · 2 h

Numerical Load Flow

الحل العددي لسريان الاستطاعة

Iterative solution, convergence and engineering interpretation.

LECTURE 11 · 2 h

Power-System Data & Visualization

بيانات نظم القدرة وتمثيل النتائج

Structured datasets, computational analysis and engineering visualization.

LECTURE 12 · 2 h

Integrated Computational Power-System Study

دراسة حاسوبية متكاملة لنظام قدرة

Modeling, computation, verification, visualization and interpretation.

Laboratories | المخابر

12 laboratories, 90 minutes each, aligned with the lecture sequence.

LAB 01 · 1.5 h

MATLAB Power-Engineering Refresher

مراجعة MATLAB لتطبيقات الطاقة

Complex numbers, arrays, functions, loops and plotting.

LAB 02 · 1.5 h

Daily Load-Curve Analysis

تحليل منحني الحمل اليومي

Energy, average load, peak demand and load factor.

LAB 03 · 1.5 h

AC Waveforms & Complex Power

الموجات المتناوبة والاستطاعة العقدية

Voltage/current waveforms and P, Q, S calculation.

LAB 04 · 1.5 h

Three-Phase Visualization

تمثيل النظام ثلاثي الطور

Three-phase waveforms and balanced-system calculations.

LAB 05 · 1.5 h

Capacitor-Bank Sizing

حساب بنك المكثفات

Reusable computational tool for power-factor correction.

LAB 06 · 1.5 h

Synchronous Generator Study

دراسة المولدة المتزامنة

Excitation, current, power angle and operating curves.

LAB 07 · 1.5 h

Short-Line Model

نموذج خط النقل القصير

Sending-end quantities, losses, efficiency and regulation.

LAB 08 · 1.5 h

Nominal-π & ABCD Model

نموذج π وثوابت ABCD

Medium-line model and sending/receiving conditions.

LAB 09 · 1.5 h

Y-Bus Construction

بناء مصفوفة القبولية Y-Bus

Construct the network admittance matrix from line data.

LAB 10 · 1.5 h

Small-System Load Flow

سريان الاستطاعة لشبكة صغيرة

Solve a compact load-flow case and interpret bus results.

LAB 11 · 1.5 h

Power-System Dataset Analysis

تحليل بيانات نظام القدرة

Import, process and visualize a small engineering dataset.

LAB 12 · 1.5 h

Integrated Mini Project

المشروع التطبيقي المصغر

End-to-end computational study with engineering interpretation.
